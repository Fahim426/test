01 System Overview

SmartSpare is a full-cycle ERP for spare-parts trading and distribution businesses. It manages the complete order-to-cash and procure-to-pay cycles, keeping purchasing, inventory, sales and finance in sync through shared master data and automatic document linking.

ModulePurpose
PurchaseRaise requests, obtain quotes, issue POs, receive goods, process supplier invoices
InventoryTrack real-time stock levels, transfers between warehouses, and stock adjustments
SalesManage enquiries, quotations, proforma invoices, sales orders, delivery and invoicing
FinanceChart of accounts, journal vouchers, AR/AP ledgers, payments, landed costs, reports
AdminCompany setup, users and roles, master data (products, customers, suppliers, dimensions)

Technology stack: React front-end (Classic UI + Modern UI) · Node.js / Express REST API · MySQL database via Prisma ORM.

Key Concepts

  • Every transaction is a document (PO, GRN, Sales Order, Invoice…). Documents link to each other, creating a full audit trail.
  • Master data (products, customers, suppliers, warehouses) is set up once in Admin and shared across all modules.
  • Roles control which modules and actions each user can access.
  • Stock levels update automatically when documents are confirmed — no manual journal is needed for routine movements.

02 Getting Started

2.1 Logging In

  1. Open the SmartSpare URL in your browser.
  2. Enter your Email and Password, then click Sign In.
  3. The server verifies your credentials and issues a session token, then redirects you to the home dashboard.
  4. If your account has limited permissions, menu items outside your role will not appear.

2.2 Switching UI Mode

SmartSpare ships two interface styles:

  • Classic UI — ribbon-based toolbar, familiar to desktop ERP users.
  • Modern UI — top-menu navigation with dark mode support.

Use the Switch UI toggle on the login screen or the top bar to change modes. Both UIs share the same data and permissions.

03 Administration & Setup

Complete these steps before transacting. Settings here flow into every module.

3.1 Company Setup

Admin → System → Company

Fill in company name, logo, address, default currency and fiscal year. These values appear on all printed documents.

3.2 Users & Roles

Admin → System → Users

  1. Create a user record with name, email and a temporary password.
  2. Assign a Role (e.g. Purchaser, Storekeeper, Sales Executive, Accountant, Admin).
  3. Roles control which modules and actions the user can access.

Admin → Products

Each product record holds: part number, description, brand, unit of measure, default warehouse, cost method and reorder level. Products are shared across Purchase, Inventory and Sales.

04 Purchase Module

The procurement cycle moves from an internal need through to a posted supplier invoice.

Purchase Request→ RFQ→ Purchase Order→ Goods Receipt→ Purchase Invoice→ AP Payment

4.1 Purchase Request (PR)

Purchase → Purchase Requests → New

Raised by any department to signal a material requirement. Fields: requested items, quantities, required date, requesting department. Once approved, a PR can be converted to an RFQ.

4.2 Request for Quotation (RFQ)

Purchase → RFQ → New  (or convert from PR)

Sent to one or more suppliers to invite price offers. Record supplier responses against each line, select the winning bid, then convert to a Purchase Order.

4.3 Purchase Order (PO)

Purchase → Purchase Orders → New  (or convert from RFQ)

Formal authorisation to buy. Contains supplier, delivery address, payment terms and line items (product, qty, unit price).

StatusMeaning
DraftCreated, not yet sent to supplier
ApprovedAuthorised and transmitted
Partially ReceivedSome lines received via GRN
Fully ReceivedAll lines received
ClosedInvoiced and complete
Link to Sales: When a Sales Order is confirmed but stock is insufficient, a PO is raised against that SO, linking customer demand directly to procurement. See section 9.3.

4.4 Goods Receipt (GRN)

Purchase → Purchase Receipts → New  (or receive from PO)

Record items physically received against the PO. Confirming a GRN automatically increases stock in the selected warehouse. Partial receipts are supported; the PO status updates to Partially Received.

4.5 Purchase Invoice

Purchase → Purchase Invoices → New  (or link from GRN)

Supplier's bill matched to the PO and GRN. Posting the invoice creates an Accounts Payable (AP) entry in Finance. Three-way match: PO quantity · GRN quantity · Invoice quantity must agree.

4.6 Purchase Return

Purchase → Purchase Returns → New

Raise a return against a posted GRN for defective or excess goods. Automatically reduces stock and creates a supplier credit in AP.

4.7 Landed Cost

Purchase → Landed Cost → New

Allocate additional import costs (freight, customs, insurance) across a GRN's lines. Adjusts the unit cost of the received stock and posts to the relevant Finance accounts.

05 Inventory Module

Inventory reflects the real-time quantity and location of every product.

5.1 Stock Enquiry

Inventory → Stock Levels

View on-hand, reserved and available quantities per product per warehouse. Filter by brand, warehouse or product category.

  • On-hand: physical quantity in the warehouse.
  • Reserved: quantity allocated to confirmed Sales Orders but not yet dispatched.
  • Available: on-hand minus reserved — the quantity free to sell.

5.2 Stock Adjustment

Inventory → Adjustments → New

Correct stock after a physical count or write-off. Each line records the reason (count variance, damage, write-off). Posts a corresponding finance journal if a cost account is configured.

5.3 Stock Transfer

Inventory → Transfers → New

Move stock between warehouses (e.g. Main Store → Bonded). The source warehouse decreases and the destination increases simultaneously. Transfers can be held in-transit until confirmed at the destination.

5.4 Stock Reservation

Confirming a Sales Order automatically reserves the required quantity. Reserved stock appears in the Stock Enquiry but cannot be allocated to another order. The reservation is released when delivery is confirmed or the order is cancelled.

06 Sales Module

The sales cycle runs from the first customer contact through to a posted invoice.

Enquiry→ Quotation→ Proforma→ Sales Order→ Pick→ Pack→ Delivery→ Sales Invoice→ AR Receipt

6.1 Enquiry

Sales → Enquiries → New

Log a customer's initial request for products or pricing. Attach notes, files or a vehicle/equipment reference. Convert to a Quotation when ready to price.

6.2 Quotation

Sales → Quotations → New  (or convert from Enquiry)

Formal price offer to the customer with a validity date. Line items pull from the Product master; pricing can be overridden. Convert to a Proforma Invoice or directly to a Sales Order.

6.3 Proforma Invoice

Sales → Proforma Invoices → New  (or convert from Quotation)

Pre-payment document issued before the order is confirmed. Used when advance payment or formal customer approval is required. Convert to a Sales Order once payment is received or approval is given.

6.4 Sales Order (SO)

Sales → Sales Orders → New  (or convert from Quotation / Proforma)

Confirmed order. Confirming the SO reserves stock in the warehouse. If stock is insufficient, raise a Purchase Order linked to this SO (see section 9.3).

StatusMeaning
DraftCreated, not yet confirmed
ConfirmedStock reserved
PickingWarehouse is picking items
PackedItems packed, ready to ship
DeliveredGoods dispatched
InvoicedSales invoice posted

6.5 Picking

Sales → Picking → New  (from confirmed SO)

Warehouse staff select the physical items from storage. The picking slip lists product, bin location and quantity to pick.

6.6 Packing

Sales → Packing → New  (from picked SO)

Items are packed; a packing list is generated per shipment or box. Weight and dimensions can be recorded for freight calculation.

6.7 Delivery

Sales → Delivery → New  (from packed SO)

Delivery note issued to the driver or courier. Confirming delivery reduces on-hand stock and releases the reservation.

6.8 Sales Invoice

Sales → Sales Invoices → New  (or auto-create from Delivery)

Posts an Accounts Receivable (AR) entry in Finance. Tax is calculated per line; totals print on the invoice PDF.

6.9 Sales Return

Sales → Sales Returns → New

Raise a return against a posted Sales Invoice. Creates a credit note in AR and returns stock to the warehouse.

07 Finance Module

Finance captures every monetary event generated by Purchase and Sales, plus manual entries.

7.1 Chart of Accounts

Finance → Accounts → Chart of Accounts

Define the GL structure: Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Expenses. Each account has a code, name, type and normal balance. Accounts are linked to suppliers (AP control), customers (AR control) and stock.

7.2 Journal Vouchers

Finance → Vouchers → New

Manual double-entry journals for opening balances, corrections and accruals. Debit and credit sides must balance before posting. Auto-vouchers are created by Purchase Invoices, Sales Invoices, Payments and Stock Adjustments.

7.3 Accounts Payable (AP)

Finance → Parties → Suppliers

Tracks the balance owed to each supplier. Populated automatically by posted Purchase Invoices and Purchase Returns. The aged AP report shows balances by 30 / 60 / 90 / 90+ days.

7.4 Accounts Receivable (AR)

Finance → Parties → Customers

Tracks the balance owed by each customer. Populated automatically by posted Sales Invoices and Sales Returns. The aged AR report shows balances by 30 / 60 / 90 / 90+ days.

7.5 Payments

Finance → Vouchers → Payment / Receipt

Record a payment to a supplier (clears AP) or a receipt from a customer (clears AR). Allocate against specific invoices or leave as an on-account balance. Bank reconciliation is performed against the bank account ledger.

09 Cross-Module Flows

9.1 Procure-to-Pay (P2P)

Purchase Request→ RFQ→ Purchase Order→ Goods Receipt→ Purchase Invoice→ AP Payment

At each step the previous document is linked, creating a full audit trail from need to payment.

9.2 Order-to-Cash (O2C)

Enquiry→ Quotation→ Proforma→ Sales Order→ Pick → Pack→ Delivery→ Sales Invoice→ AR Receipt

9.3 SO-Triggered Purchase Order

When a Sales Order is confirmed and available stock is below the required quantity:

  1. User raises a Purchase Order from within the SO screen.
  2. The PO is linked to the SO by reference.
  3. Goods are received against the PO — stock increases.
  4. Picking can now proceed against the SO.

This ensures customer demand drives procurement directly, with full traceability between the customer order and the supplier order.
